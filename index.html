      Install

      Three steps

      1. Download the zip from your receipt and unzip it. Your licence key is in the same email.
      2. Drag the .vst3 files into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
      3. In FL Studio, open Options → Manage plugins and click Find plugins. They show up under FFMB Joey. Open one and paste your key to activate.

      Questions

      FAQ

      What's the difference between buying one plugin and the pack?

      A single plugin gets you that one plugin. The pack gets you all nine for far less than buying them one by one.

      Do the plugins work together?

      Yes. Dungeon Key finds the key of your beat and sends it to Dungeon Tune and Dungeon Doubler, so the autotune and harmonies are always in the right key. They also share one look, so once you know one you know all of them.

      Does Dungeon Tune do the hard, robotic autotune sound?

      That's its default. HARD mode snaps every note instantly and dead flat, and the HEAVY knob takes it from a clean snap to a full robot voice. CLASSIC mode is there for natural tuning.

      Will they make my mix lag?

      Every plugin tells FL Studio its latency, so everything stays lined up automatically. Dungeon Tune also has a Low Latency mode (about 13 ms) for recording through it.

      How do licence keys work?

      Your key arrives in your receipt email. Open the plugin, paste the key and click Activate. A Dungeon Pack key unlocks all nine plugins on that computer at once. Getting a new computer? Click LICENSED in the corner of any plugin and deactivate the old one, then activate the key on the new one.

      Is there a Mac version?

      Not yet. Right now the plugins are Windows VST3 only.
